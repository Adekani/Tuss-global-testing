Frequently Asked Questions

We want to make sure you have complete confidence before registering.

Is this event really free?

Yes, it is 100% free to attend. We are supported by community donations and partnerships. Our primary purpose is maternal safety and wholeness, so there are absolutely no fees or hidden upsells during this session.

I cannot attend live. Will there be a replay?

Yes, but you must register! We only email recording files, downloadable sheets, and PDF workbook material to users registered through our Supabase system.

Will my responses in the form remain private?

Absolutely. Your personal selections, questions, and circumstances are completely confidential. We only analyze collective trends (e.g., "60% of our registered moms are currently experiencing postpartum burnouts") so our speakers can adjust their presentation to address your exact challenges.
