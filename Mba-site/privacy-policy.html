Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Maison Blanche is African (MBisAfrican) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use this website.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly through our forms, including your name, business details, and other information needed to process your inquiry or application.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, process vendor applications, and communicate with you about our services.

Analytics and advertising tools

We use Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use our website and to measure the performance of our advertising. We may add Google Analytics in the future.

How we store your information

Your information is stored in our own private database. We do not store your information using third-party form tools.

Payments

If you make a payment to us, it is processed securely through Paystack or Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details ourselves.

Sharing your information

We share information with third parties only where necessary to provide our services to you, and only as agreed with you, such as with warehousing or fulfillment partners when relevant to your order.

Communication tools

We may use tools such as Zoom to conduct calls with you as part of our services.

Data Access and Deletion Requests

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you or request that we delete your data from our systems. To submit a request, please contact us at the email address below.

Questions about our privacy practices?

Reach out to our team directly for any clarification or data requests.
